About the Symposium

CONVERGENCE 2026 offers an exciting opportunity in both offline and online mode, to explore emerging global research frontiers at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience, neurotechnology, nanotechnology, and artificial intelligence. Delegates will gain insights from eminent experts, discover cutting-edge research, present their work through scientific poster sessions, and engage in interactive roundtable discussions on future research priorities and challenges.

The symposium will provide a vibrant platform for knowledge exchange, interdisciplinary networking, industry-academia interaction, and building meaningful research collaborations, while helping participants identify new opportunities in a rapidly evolving global research landscape.

Official Guidelines

Abstract Submission Guidelines

Participants who wish to present their research at Convergence 2026 are invited to submit an abstract through the symposium registration form.

Abstract Requirements

  • • The abstract should not exceed 250 words.
  • • The abstract must be submitted in PDF format.
  • • Only one abstract file should be uploaded.
  • • Please follow the formatting and identification instructions provided in the registration form.
  • • Abstracts should be relevant to one or more of the scientific themes of Convergence 2026.
