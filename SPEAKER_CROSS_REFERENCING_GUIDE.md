## 2. Speaker Announcement Template (For Deepak Sharma / JDF Website)

*Speakers can publish this clean announcement on their organization's blog, news feed, or personal portfolio:*

```markdown
### Speaking at CONVERGENCE 2026: Emerging Frontiers in Nano, Bio-Info, Cogno and AI

I am honored to deliver an invited talk at **CONVERGENCE 2026** (National Symposium on Emerging Frontiers in Nano, Bio-Info, Cogno and Artificial Intelligence), hosted by my alma mater, the **Center For Converging Technologies (CCT), University of Rajasthan, Jaipur** on **November 28–29, 2026** (in hybrid mode).

- **Talk Title:** *Startup Engineering for Social Impact*
- **Case Study Spotlight:** *Nurolab — Interdisciplinary EEG, AI, and Mental Healthcare Diagnostics*

Looking forward to connecting with faculty, researchers, and students across India exploring deep-tech translational research.

- **Symposium Portal:** [CONVERGENCE 2026](https://convergence26.github.io) (or [Google Site](https://sites.google.com/view/cct-convergence-2026/))
```
